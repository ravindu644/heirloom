Three rooms for Study club

One room, three layouts, the same afternoon: maya with sam, lina and dev, on Thursday the 26th. Each phone frame is 412 by 915 and scrolls inside, as the app does. Pick the one that feels like your room.

In all three, Code and settings opens the room’s sheet: the code and Copy the code, Leave the room, and for lina, its keeper, the name, the target, the order and removing a member.

A

Faces first. The four of you in the order you are working, each with their time left; the room’s week, its streak and the notes come after them, and a called lap waits at the foot, in reach of a thumb.

What it gives up

On a phone the room’s week and streak sit under the four people, so seeing how the room is doing takes a scroll. While a lap is called, the call holds the foot of the screen.

B

The room is one runner. Its week against last week and its streak lead, drawn as Progress draws yours; the people follow as a short list, and a called lap is a dark strip between the two.

What it gives up

Who is on a lap right now, the thing a room is opened for, starts under two panels on a phone. The chart is the one on Progress, so the room can read as a second Progress.

C

One track. Everyone is a lane on today’s clock: laps, breaks, the line of now, and the called lap marked at 15:00 ahead of it, so the room reads as one race with a start to line up for.

What it gives up

The most to draw and keep right: a lane for each person, ticks for now and for the call. On a phone four hours fit across, so a lap is a small block, and a friend who is away is an empty track.
