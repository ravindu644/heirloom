The shape
A task with subtasks has the arrow where a task has its box; a task with none keeps its box. The control column centres on 28px from the row's left, and everything else starts on 56px: the title, the line under it, each subtask's words and the field that adds one.
Closed shows
"2 of 4 subtasks" in words on the project's line. The squares stay the task's laps, at the right end as now: a second row of squares beside them would read as more laps, and two bare "x of y" on one row would say of what by place alone.
Adding
Yes, Jira's way: a field, "Add a subtask", at the foot of the open list, with "Add" inside it from the first letter. The new one lands just above it, the keyboard stays up for the next, and no bar speaks, as the task's sheet already does. A task without subtasks gets its first one in its sheet (tap the words), where the field is today; from then on it has the arrow.
Laps and the rest
The estimate, laps, project, due day, time and Start stay the task's, on its own row, open or closed; the Next card keeps its outline and Start. A lap is on the task, never a subtask: a lap that named a step would need the session to carry it, a contract change.
Order
Press and hold a subtask to move it among its own, as rows are moved now (each already has its order). Not in or out of a task: a carried row that could land either beside or inside another would make every carry a guess. Making a subtask a task, or the reverse, is left for later.
Opening one
Leaves the others open, as A1: closing a row above would move the one just tapped. Open stays open on this device until closed; a task done leaves Today with its subtasks.
While its lap runs
The task is in the strip at the top and nowhere else on Today, as now; its title opens its sheet, where its subtasks are ticked.
