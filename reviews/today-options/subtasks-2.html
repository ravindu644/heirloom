What is decided

Ticking the task
Its own box, at the left, as every task has. It finishes the task whatever its subtasks say; open ones go with it as they are. Ticking every subtask finishes nothing but them.
Opening
"2 of 4 subtasks" on the line under the title is a link in a line with an arrow after it: down closed, up open. It starts on the words' edge (56) when the task has no project, after the project when it has one.
Closed shows
How many subtasks are done, with its noun; the squares stay the task's laps. Every one ticked and the task not: "4 of 4 subtasks" beside an empty box, still in its place on the list, Next still Next.
Open on Today
The subtasks, each with its box on the box column (28) and its words on 56, struck through when done and kept where they stand. No field, no editing, no Remove.
In the sheet
The subtasks as words with no box: tap a title to change it in place, "Remove" takes one out and says so where it stood with Undo (a bar would lie under the sheet), press and hold to move one, and "Add a subtask" at the foot. "Mark done" there still finishes the task.
Close second
The arrow at the end of the title's line, as in A2: on the phone's Next card it stands between the title and Start and leaves the title about 170px.
Opening one
Leaves the others open, as A1.
Contract
None. A subtask is a task with `parent`; its `done`, `title`, `order` and `deleted` are cells it already has. The rule "a subtask done ends nothing" already holds.
