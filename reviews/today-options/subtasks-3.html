Subtasks on Today: the strip, and a new way to open them

Saturday 28 November, 14:20. Drawn with heirloom's own colours, type and kit.

DecidedR1: the running task's subtasks under Today's strip

The shape
One surface hangs from the strip. Closed it is one row: the fold arrow in the box column (centred 28px in) and "2 of 4 subtasks" on 56. Open, a row per subtask, its box on 28 and its words on 56, struck through where it stands when done. Only ticking: making and changing stay in the task's sheet. Focus does not change.
The strip
Its dot is centred 28px in and its time starts on 56, so the surface under it lines up with it. It was 20 and 36.
Paused
The strip says Resume and keeps its subtasks.
The lap ends, a break
The task goes back to its place in the list, its row open if the strip's was. In a break the strip is the break's ("4:12 Short break") and holds no subtasks.
No subtasks
Nothing new appears: the strip is as it is now.
Undo
The bar, as now: "Done: Main content with headings in order. 3 of 4 subtasks."

A new way to open a task's subtasks

My pick is S2, the band. It gives the opener what Habits' arrow has, the box column and a whole row to itself, by giving it a row of its own under the task, so the task's box keeps the line above. It is also already the strip's tray, so Today opens subtasks one way everywhere and R1 does not change.

In all three the task's box stays at the left (centre 28, words on 56), ticking happens only on Today, editing only in the sheet, and Jira's rule holds: "3 of 3 subtasks" beside an empty box is a task still open.

S1

The arrow at the right end, as Habits' at the left

The opener
A 48px arrow at the row's right end, its centre 28px from the right as the box's is from the left. The count is plain words on the line under the title, "1 of 3 subtasks".
Not the box
It stands at the other end of the row, has no square round it, and is the same mark Habits opens with.
Not Start
Start is a filled word; the arrow stands after it, bare, on the card's edge.
Its cost
On a phone's Next card the title shares its line with Start and the arrow, about 170px.
In the tray
The tray's row turns to match: the count on 56, the arrow at its right end.
S2

My pick The band: a row of its own at the card's foot

The opener
Under a rule at the card's foot, a full-width row 48px tall: the arrow in the box column, "1 of 3 subtasks" on 56. The whole band is the target. Open, the subtasks follow it on the same two edges, so it reads as the first line of the list it opens.
Not the box
It stands on its own line under a rule; the task's box is on the task's line above, and an arrow is not a square.
Not Start
Start stays on the task's line, a filled word; the band holds no button.
Its cost
A task with subtasks is 48px taller, closed.
In the tray
The same row, as decided: the tray already is a band.
S3

The subtasks in miniature

The opener
A line on 56 under the title: one small box per subtask, filled when done, and the arrow after them. The line is the target, 48px tall by padding. What opens is the same boxes at full size.
Not the box
They are a third of its size, several in a row, on a line of their own under the words.
Not Start
Start is a filled word at the row's end; the small boxes sit under the title, far from it.
Its cost
No number is written, and a task of 12 subtasks is a long line of small boxes; a screen reader hears "1 of 3 subtasks".
In the tray
The tray's row becomes the same small boxes and arrow, on 56.
