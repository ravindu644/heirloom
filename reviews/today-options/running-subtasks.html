The running task's subtasks

My pick is R3: the same subtasks, the same way, wherever the running task is shown. On Today they hang under the strip; on Focus they are a panel under the race. One open or closed state for the task, so the strip, Focus and the task's own row after the lap always agree; and it answers a rule already learned: a thing that shows a running state offers what can be done in it, wherever it shows.

In every option

The shape
The subtasks of the running task stand in one surface. Closed it is one row: the fold arrow in the box column (centred 28px in) and "2 of 4 subtasks" on 56. Open, a row per subtask, its box on 28 and its words on 56, struck through where it stands when done. Only ticking: making and changing stay in the task's sheet.
No subtasks
Nothing new appears: the strip is as it is today, and Focus has no panel.
Paused
As running: the strip says Resume and keeps its subtasks; Focus greys the digits and keeps them.
The lap ends
On Today the task goes back to its place in the list, and its own row is open if the strip's was. On Focus the panel stays: the task stays Focus's until another is chosen.
A break
On Today the strip is the break's ("4:12 Short break") and holds no subtasks; they are on the task's row. On Focus the panel stays through the break, the likeliest moment to tick what the lap finished.
Undo
On Today, the bar as now: "Done: Main content with headings in order. 3 of 4 subtasks." On Focus the box is its own undo, as on Habits: a bar there would lie over Pause and Stop.
The strip
Moves onto the two edges: its dot is centred 28px in and the time starts on 56, so the surface under it lines up with it. It was 20 and 36.
Contract
None. These are the same subtask cells, shown in one more place.

R1

Under Today's strip

The subtasks hang from the strip, where the user looked for them. Focus stays as it is. Its cost: on the phone, during a lap, Focus is the screen you are on, and the subtasks are one tab away.

R2

On Focus, under the race

The subtasks are a panel on the screen of the lap, after the race on the phone and in the rail on the laptop. Today's strip stays as it is, and its clock goes to Focus. Its cost: on Today the user's question gets no answer on the screen they asked it on, only a way to another.

R3

My pick Both, the same way

R1's pictures on Today and R2's on Focus, together, with one state: open on the strip is open on Focus and on the task's row after the lap. Its cost: two places draw the same rows, so they are built once in the kit and used twice.
