Progress: what today counted

Pick C. It answers "what did I do today" with the tasks you did and the minutes each took, adding up to one total, and a full day of 8 laps on 3 tasks is still three lines and a total.

A is the safe one if the hour-by-hour lane should stay. B is the most like heirloom's race (splits that add up as you read), but a full day makes it the longest.

  • In all three: "Looking back on today" is gone, and the day is one panel with one heading.
  • Every figure counts today only. The lap from 23:46 to 00:31 adds its 31 minutes after midnight, and its row says the other 14 count on Friday, so 31 and 16 make the 47 that is shown.
  • Breaks are never counted as focus. A lap taken out of the count keeps its place and says "not counted"; "Don't count it" and "Count it again" stay where the lap's times are.
  • Each option is drawn three ways on a phone (the day from your screenshot, a full day, a day with nothing yet), then the same day at a laptop's width.
