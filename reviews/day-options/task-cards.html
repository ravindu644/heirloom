Today, lap by lap: a card per task

Pick A. Each task's whole day is one card that is three lines closed, and read down the cards' slivers still make the day's lane.

  • In all three, "done at" is never a row of its own: it is in the task's card. The running split is gone, because a card holds laps from different hours. The figure at the top is the sum of the cards' times.
  • Your day, as your phone drew it: 8 laps, 3 tasks. The minutes here are counted from the times shown, so the cards add up to 2h 41m. Your phone said 2h 42m because it rounds the day's seconds once; see the note under the options.
  • The lap from 23:45 on Friday gives today only its 32 minutes after midnight: "23:45 Fri to 00:32, 32 of 47 minutes today".

What the others do, and what this takes

  • Toggl Track's "Group similar time entries" folds entries of one task and project on one day into one line that opens. Taken: one line per task that opens to its entries. Left: its newest-first list of single entries.
  • Timing (Mac) lists the day's projects from most time to least beside the timeline. Taken for C: most time first. Left: the timeline as a second picture beside the list.
  • Rize puts the day on a vertical timeline with projects in a column beside it, and hovering shows a breakdown. Left: hover does not exist on a phone, and two columns of one day is the day twice.
  • Focus To-Do's report draws today's focus as a Gantt chart and counts sessions and finished tasks. Taken for A: a task's own lane on the day's hours. Left: its chart beside a separate list.
  • Session lists past sessions by day and filters them by category. Left: a filter where a card per task already groups them.
A

A card per task with its own sliver, laps folded behind a band.The pick

My pick: it keeps what B2 got right (read down the slivers and you see the day's lane), and a closed card is three lines however many laps it holds.

Order
By each task's first lap of the day, so the cards still read in the order the day began. Laps for no task are one card, "Nothing in particular", placed by its first lap like any other.
At the right
The task's time today in `figure-md`, under one "Task's time" heading. The running split goes: cards are not in time order inside, so a running total would add the same task twice. The figure at the top is the sum of the cards.
Don't count it
Open the band ("4 laps") and each lap is one line: its square (filled as far as it ran, hollow when not counted), its facts, and the link at the right end, always there. A lap not counted stays in its card, hollow on the sliver and in its square, says "not counted", and leaves the task's time.
Done
An ink tick on the sliver at the moment, and the card's summary ends "Done at 18:14." A task done with no lap today is a card with only the tick and "No lap today. Done at 09:10." Ticked, unticked and ticked again, only the tick that stands is drawn.
Breaks
Only the break a lap of this task earned, right after it on the sliver, in `rest`. Never a line of words.
Folding
A card of two laps or more is closed by default: the head, the sliver and the summary already say the task's day. The band follows DESIGN.md's rule: a row of its own under a `hair`, `touch` tall, the arrow in the box column (centre 28px), the words on 56px. Open, it reads "Each lap". A card of one lap has nothing to fold: its one line stands where the band would.

Gives up: a lap is a tap away instead of in sight, and the clock order of the day lives only on the slivers.

B

A card per task as its own short log, always open.

Order
Latest first: the task you worked on last is on top, as Toggl lists entries. "Nothing in particular" is placed by its latest lap.
At the right
The task's time today. No running split, for the same reason as A; the figure at the top is the sum of the cards.
Don't count it
On every lap line, at its right end, always in sight. A lap not counted keeps its line with "not counted" and "Count it again".
Done
The card's last line, "Done at 18:14", with the box's tick drawn before it, so the task's day ends where it ended. A task done with no lap: a card of that one line. Done twice: one line, the tick that stands.
Breaks
Only a break that sits between two laps of this task, as a quiet line between them. A break before another task's lap is not this card's.
Folding
Never folded. A card past 5 laps would show its latest 5 and one line for the rest, as the day does today.

Gives up: no picture of the day at all, and the link stands on every line, eight times on your day.

C

A card per task like a receipt line, most time first; the card opens its laps in a sheet.

Order
Most time first, so the card on top is where the day went. "Nothing in particular" and tasks done with no lap come last.
At the right
The task's time today; the cards add up to the figure, top to bottom, like a receipt. No running split.
Don't count it
Tap the card: the task's sheet lists each lap with its facts and the link at the right end. The sheet says what the tap did, in the sheet, with "Count it again".
Done
In the card's summary, "Done at 18:14." Done with no lap: a card with no bar and "No lap today. Done at 09:10."
Breaks
None. The bar is the task's laps end to end, each as long as its minutes, a hollow one for a lap not counted. No clock, so no breaks.
Folding
Nothing folds in the list; the sheet holds the laps.

Gives up: when things happened, until the sheet is open, and a sheet is the heaviest way to reach one link.

The one rule all three need: what a minute is

The splits said "the day's focus, rounded once", and each row's minutes were the difference of its shown times. The two could differ by one, which is why your phone ended on 2h 42m while its rows add to 2h 41m. With cards the sum is in plain sight, so one rule is needed. My suggestion: a lap's minutes are the difference of its shown times, rounded to the nearest minute, and every total adds those up: the card, the figure at the top, the year grid and Focus. The other way, rounding the day once and dealing the odd minutes to the cards with the largest remainders, keeps today's figure but lets a card say one more than its lines.
