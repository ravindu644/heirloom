Today, lap by lap: a clean lap row

Pick 1. Every lap reads as the same three lines and nothing else, and Don't count it comes where the finger already is, in the row it acts on, then goes again.

  • In all three, every lap row has one order: what it was and "Day so far", the sliver, then one line of facts. A note, a link or a moment never comes between them.
  • Midnight is said in the facts, not a sentence: "23:47 Fri to 00:31, 31 of 44 minutes today".
  • A task done is a moment, never a line inside a lap's card. 1 and 3 give it a row of its own; 2 folds it into the lap it ended.
  • The splits add up: 31m, then 47m, then 55m with the running lap, and 55m is the figure at the top. Your phone shows 23:45 with 13 minutes on Friday, and 11:03 to 11:18 as 15 minutes against a split of 16. The times and minutes there are rounded from different seconds. They are drawn here as 23:47 and 11:19 so they agree.
1

A lap opens in place.The pick

Every lap row is the same three lines: what, the sliver, the facts. "Day so far" sits at the right of the first line. Nothing else lives in a row until it is tapped.

Don't count it: a tap on a lap tints it (`you-tint`, the selected row) and opens one quiet button under its facts. A second tap, or a tap on another lap, closes it. After the tap the bar says what happened, with Undo, and the open row now offers "Count it again".

Moments: "Morning Routine 🙏 done at 11:19" is a row of its own, one `label` line between the same rules as the laps, with no sliver and no figure, so it never reads as part of the card above.

Midnight: folded into the facts, "23:47 Fri to 00:31, 31 of 44 minutes today". No sentence under it.

Gives up: nothing on the row says it can be tapped until someone does. The tint and the button arrive together, so a first tap teaches it.

2

One fixed place at the row's end.

Every lap row is the same grid: what and "Day so far" on the first line, the sliver, then the facts with the link at the right end of that line. The running lap leaves the place empty. A long facts line wraps under itself and never pushes the link.

Don't count it: always in sight, always in the same corner, under the figure. The tap acts at once, and the bar says what happened, with Undo.

Moments: a task done as its lap ended is part of that lap. A finish tick stands on the sliver and the facts end "Task done." A task done at any other time is a quiet row of its own, as in 1.

Midnight: folded into the facts, as in 1.

Gives up: the link is on every row, so a full day shows it five times, and it sits one thumb away from the next lap's.

3

The lap's own sheet.

Every lap row is the same three lines as in 1, and the whole row opens the lap's sheet, the way a task row opens its sheet on Today.

Don't count it: a secondary button in the sheet, with the sentence that says what it does above it. The answer is said in the sheet, where eyes are, and the same button turns to "Count it again".

Moments: a row on the lane. A `label` line and a bare sliver carrying one ink tick at its time, so reading down the slivers the moment is a mark in the day, not a line in a card.

Midnight: the row says "23:47 Fri to 00:31, 31 of 44 minutes today". The full sentence lives in the sheet, where there is room.

Gives up: a sheet is the heaviest way to reach one button. It earns its place only if a lap gets more in it later (its task, its two devices).
