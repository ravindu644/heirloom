# Focus To-Do: what it was, what it charged for, why it worked 🍅

> [!TIP]
> Focus To-Do is a Pomodoro timer and task list from SuperElement (Shenzhen) with 10M+ Play
> downloads. What made it stick for students was not the timer. It was the reports ("It makes me
> feel proud to see what I've accomplished") and small code-joined groups that ranked friends by
> focus time and showed who was studying right now. What drove people away: a 3-day trial that
> silently locked the timer length, sync and reports, sync that failed even for paying users, and
> lost data.

Checked 2026-11-24. Every claim below was seen in a saved copy of its source, unless it carries
⚠️ (reported by users only) or "inferred". Sources and the fact-check table are in
[references.md](references.md).

## 1. Facts to remember

| | |

| Maker | SuperElement Soft on Play; "Shenzhen Tomato Software Technology Co., Ltd." on the App Store |
| Size | 4.6 stars from 290K reviews and 10M+ downloads on Play (updated Nov 11, 2026); 4.8 from 14,757 ratings on iOS |

| Trial | About 3 days of premium on install, then features lock (users repeatedly say they were not told) |

## 2. Every feature, grouped

**Timer**
- Pomodoro with configurable work, short break and long break. Custom lengths lock after the trial ⚠️.
- Full-screen timer and extending time during a pomodoro (iOS 8.6, 2024-10-09).
- Count of estimated pomodoros per task ("Estimate the workload or set a goal").
- White noise and alarm sounds, most of them premium ⚠️.
- Strict mode and an app whitelist; app blocking during a pomodoro (iOS 8.7, 2025).
- A floating timer window on Android with adjustable opacity (Nov 2026).

**Tasks**
- Projects, subtasks, due dates, reminders, repeating tasks, notes, tags and folders.
- Reminders, repeats, tags and folders are premium ⚠️.

**Reports** (the part students praise most)
- Gantt chart of focus time, trend chart of completed tasks and focus time, time split per
  project, daily, weekly, monthly and yearly reports, a calendar view, a day timeline of sessions.
- A daily focus goal, set in whole hours only ⚠️.
- A report history page and a monthly focus widget (iOS 8.9, 2026-11-13).
- No heatmap. A user asked for one in January 2025: "wish it also had a heat map and would show
  the longest streak you have met your study hrs goal just like in anki".
- No achievements or badges were found.

**Groups and social**
- Join a group with a group code. Public groups exist; one user estimated a 100-member group was
  "probably having 80 bot users" ⚠️.
- Members see "who else is currently studying" and "each person's cumulative focus hours for the
  day" (a 2026 review of the desktop and extension version).
- Ranking by focus time: by week, by the last 30 days (iOS 8.4, 2023-10-27), and a global top 10.
  Tree size is ranked too.
- A basic group chat with no notifications. The App Store rating says "Contains Messaging and Chat".
- Only the top 3 ranked members can edit a group, and empty groups delete themselves (developer
  reply, 2020-01-06). Removing members arrived in iOS 8.4.
- Social features can be hidden (three 2026 reviews).
- No shared tasks or shared projects were found anywhere. Groups shared focus time and presence,
  not work.

**Forest**
- Completed pomodoros and tasks produce "sunlight" (in grams) that you tap to collect. It grows a
  plant through levels. Users report daily caps of 800 g or 480 g (sources disagree) and a 40 g
  bonus for sharing ⚠️.
- A daily login streak boosts sunlight. The streak counts logins, not study goals (inferred).

**Sync**
- Account sync across all platforms, premium only. Premium was sold per platform (developer reply,
  2018-09-28: "we sold by platform ... android, iPhone, Mac, Windows").
- A running timer does not show on other devices (Play review, 2024-03-15).

## 3. What users loved

From the 2,000 newest Play reviews (Aug 2025 to Nov 2026), counted by keyword (rough):

1. **Studying and exams:** 261 mentions, 240 of them 4 to 5 stars.
2. **White noise and sounds:** 69 mentions.
3. **Statistics and reports:** 68 mentions. "It's satisfying to see all the past task done and the
   hours of effort you put" (iOS, 2021).
4. **No ads:** 14 explicit mentions. One ADHD user argues ads "would be a distraction".
5. **Groups as friendly competition:** "the group function helps me to be more motivated (trying
   to be the first among me friends)" (Play, 2021-01-22).
6. **Lifetime price instead of a subscription:** "reasonable one-and-done payment".
7. **The bell:** "the sound of the bell really does ping my brain to move to the next task".

## 4. What users hated

1. **The paywall after the trial:** 178 of the 2,000 recent reviews mention premium, subscription
   or trial; 79 of those are 1 or 2 stars. The most common: custom timer length is locked.
2. **Paid and not unlocked, no reply from support:** six dated reports from 2023 to 2026.
3. **Sync failures, even for paying users:** "Different information in each device" (2021).
4. **Data loss:** "ALL my data, notes, tasks were GONE" after a daylight-saving change (iOS,
   2025-11-10). Free users lose data on reinstall because cloud backup is premium.
5. **Timer stops in the background, alarms that do not ring:** reports in 2024 and 2026.
6. **Streaks that reset themselves:** "150+ day streak and it erased itself" (iOS, 2022).
7. **Wrong group hours:** "it was showing 29 hours in group and people were asking me that am i a
   bot" (Play, 2026-03-20).
8. **Nagging:** review pop-ups after every timer, report notifications that "will not go away".
9. **Clutter:** "Nice app, cluttered interface".
10. **A broken update in October 2026:** "you've vibecoded that last update?"

## 5. What we take, what we leave

| Take | Why |
|---|---|
| Pomodoro estimates per task, done vs estimated | The plan and the work meet in one number |
| Reports, many of them, all free | The single most praised part |
| A heatmap and a goal-based streak | Users asked for both, by name |
| Small private groups, joined by code, showing who is focusing now and today's hours | The thing our user misses most |
| Hide-the-social switch | Long-time users value it |
| No ads, no paywall, sync for everyone | Every top complaint is a paywall or a sync failure |
| A bell that marks the change of phase | A cue users notice |

| Leave | Why |
|---|---|
| Global leaderboards and public groups | Bots, explicit images, "am i a bot" |
| Login streaks | They reward opening the app, and they reset and cause grief |
| Tap-to-collect currencies with daily caps | Built to bring you back, not to help you work |
| A chat without notifications | Half a feature. Cheers are enough (see [motivation.md](motivation.md)) |
| Running timer that stays on one device | Our user wants laptop and phone in step |
