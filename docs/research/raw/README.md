# Raw research reports

The four research agents' reports and the lead's source log, unedited except for pronouns. Each
claim is marked stated (S) or inferred (I) and names the saved page it came from.

The saved pages they cite (`pages/agent-N/...`, `sites/...`) were written to a temporary folder
during research and now live in `docs/research/pages/`, which is gitignored: they are other
people's work, kept on the machine that did the research so a claim can be re-checked without
fetching again. Paths inside the reports still point at the temporary folder; read
`.../scratchpad/research/pages/` as `docs/research/pages/`.
