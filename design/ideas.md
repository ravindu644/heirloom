# Ideas

## The defaults for this brief

- The default Pomodoro app: a ring that empties around big digits, a red accent on a dark ground, a
  tomato mascot, confetti when it ends, a GitHub-style green heatmap, a "🔥 12 day streak" chip.
- The default "Anthropic-like" app: cream ground, clay-orange accent, a serif headline, a hand-drawn
  doodle in the empty state. That is copying a brand, not taking its qualities.
- The default AI dashboard for Progress: four KPI tiles with icons in tinted circles, a gradient area
  chart, a donut of projects.

None of these is used without a reason from this student's case.

## What belongs to this case and no other

1. A pomodoro is a tomato: the kitchen timer Cirillo used was tomato shaped. Red when ripe, green
   before. The green star on top is the calyx.
2. A set is four pomodoros, then a long break. Four strokes and a fifth across is a tally gate.
3. They were motivated by "looking at my own past records". The records were the reward.
4. They studied with one friend in a group. Two people, a few at most, not a social network.
5. They are learning C, React and Next.js, and procrastinate. The first pomodoro is the hard one.
6. The timer is read from across a desk, often next to a laptop full of code.
7. They lost it because a mod broke. This one is free and self-hosted: their data is theirs.
8. "Not shame": past you is the only person you are compared with.
9. A streak that forgives: a rest day does not reset two weeks of work.
10. Phone and laptop, both from one web UI, light and dark.

## Idea sentences

Format: on this app, [something from the list] becomes [the device]. Method in brackets.

1. The tally gate a student scratches on a desk becomes the set of four: four strokes, and the long
   break strikes through them. (borrowed format)
2. The pen stroke being drawn becomes the timer: the current stroke grows from the bottom of the
   gate to the top over 25 minutes. (substitution)
3. The past records they used to look at become a ghost who runs beside them, the way a time-trial
   ghost does in a racing game. (pictorial analogy)
4. A pomodoro becomes a lap and a set becomes four laps; the day is a race against your usual day,
   reported in splits ("one pomodoro behind last Tuesday"). (borrowed format)
5. A pomodoro ripens: the whole screen turns from unripe green to tomato red over 25 minutes, read
   from across the room by colour alone. (extreme situation)
6. The ripeness of the fruit becomes the heatmap scale: a day you barely worked is green, a long day
   is red. (substitution)
7. The study room becomes a lit building at night: each friend is a window, lit while they focus.
   (pictorial analogy; rejected: a building of strangers is a social app, they have one or two friends)
8. Two desks side by side become the study room: split-screen co-op, your half and your friend's
   half of one screen. (juxtaposition; kept as the Room screen inside route B)
9. The kitchen timer's printed dial becomes a ruler of minutes sliding under a fixed pointer.
   (borrowed format; rejected: it is still a dial, and it reads worse across a desk than digits)
10. Each finished pomodoro prints a thermal ticket stub, and history is the spike they pile on.
    (borrowed format; rejected: receipts are this year's escape-from-AI costume)
11. A compiler's output becomes the end-of-day summary: "6 pomodoros, 0 warnings". (borrowed format;
    rejected: it only fits the C weeks, and jokes in the chrome get old by day three)
12. The timer has no ring and no number while you work, only the task's name, very large; the time
    appears when you look for it. (omission; rejected: the brief says read at a glance)
13. A tomato's four seed chambers become the set: a cross-section whose chambers fill. (substitution;
    rejected: cute once, and it is a mascot in disguise)

## The three routes

| Route | Risk | Idea sentence | Method |
|---|---|---|---|
| A Tally | safe | The tally gate (four strokes, one across) becomes the pomodoro set, and every count in the app is written in strokes. | pen strokes on a quiet, type-led page |
| B Pacer | adventurous | Their past records become a ghost who runs beside them on every screen; a pomodoro is a lap and the day is a race against their usual day. | race timing: lanes, splits, condensed figures |
| C Ripening | scary | A pomodoro ripens: the screen is one field that turns from green to tomato red as the 25 minutes pass. | flat screen-printed fields, three inks |

Logo test, for each: put Forest, Pomodoist or Focus To-Do in the sentence.
- A: a tally gate of four plus one only fits an app whose set is four plus a long break. Passes.
- B: Strava has segments and ghosts, but not for study time against your own weekday. Passes for
  this category; the ghost device itself is borrowed from games.
- C: Forest grows a tree, which is close in spirit (growth over time). The tomato's own colours make
  it this technique's, not any timer's. Passes narrowly.
